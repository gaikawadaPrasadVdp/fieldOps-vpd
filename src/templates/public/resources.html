ENTERPRISE PLATFORM FAQ

Frequently Asked Operational Questions

Q:

How does FieldOps Nexus connect to existing ERP platforms?

FieldOps Nexus comes with bi-directional RESTful APIs and webhooks for synchronization of inventory levels, purchase requisitions, asset cost ledgers, and workforce timecards with SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, and Microsoft Dynamics.

Q:

Is it possible to operate in offline mode without internet connection?

Yes, the FieldOps mobile application allows technicians to work on assigned tasks with no connection available as work orders, asset documentation, and checklists get saved locally. Synchronization of all signatures, photos, and status updates occurs when connection is regained.

Q:

How is Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) implemented?

FieldOps Nexus supports precise RBAC roles for Super Admin, Asset Manager, Maintenance Planner, Technician, Storekeeper, Auditor, Client, among others ensuring that only verified data is accessible by respective users.

Q:

How extensive is the audit trail available?

Major changes to work order status and progress, part allocations, signatures, and checklist entries are logged in the audit trail with timestamp and user identification information.
