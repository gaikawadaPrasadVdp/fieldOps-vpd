FieldOps Nexus Cookie & Session Management Policy

Version 2.2 • Effective: October 2026 • Technical Reference: SEC-COOK-2026

SEC-COOK-2026
This Cookie Policy explains how FieldOps Nexus uses cookies, session identifiers, and local browser storage to provide secure, authenticated enterprise services and maintain strict tenant boundaries.

1 What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text strings placed on your device by a web server when you visit or navigate web applications. They allow the platform to remember your authenticated session across requests, prevent cross-site request forgery, and enforce active organizational tenant isolation without requiring you to re-authenticate on every page navigation.

2 Types of Cookies Used on FieldOps Nexus

Because FieldOps Nexus is a business-critical enterprise SaaS platform, we prioritize security, performance, and tenant privacy over commercial tracking. We deploy strictly necessary operational cookies.

Cookie Identifier Category Duration Security Attributes Purpose & Description
sessionid Essential 14 days (or browser close) HttpOnly; SameSite=Lax; Secure Maintains the authenticated user session, tenant organization ID, and RBAC security role across requests.
csrftoken Essential 1 year SameSite=Lax; Secure Cryptographic protection token preventing Cross-Site Request Forgery attacks on state-changing forms (dispatches, work orders, logins).
messages Functional Session HttpOnly; SameSite=Lax Stores transient UI notification alerts and validation feedback across page redirects.

3 Essential & Functional Cookies

The cookies listed above are strictly necessary for the technical operation of the ERP. Without sessionid and csrftoken:

  • Users cannot securely log into the system or access tenant-specific asset management dashboards.
  • Role permissions and multi-site organization switches cannot be securely validated.
  • Web browsers cannot post safety checklist evaluations, inventory transactions, or incident reports without security rejection.

All session tokens are configured with HttpOnly flags to prevent client-side script inspection (mitigating XSS vector extraction) and SameSite=Lax to protect cross-origin requests.

4 Analytics & Tracking Policy

Zero Commercial Ad Trackers: FieldOps Nexus does not utilize third-party commercial advertising networks, social media pixels, cross-site trackers, or behavioral remarketing cookies.

Internal Server-Side Telemetry: Operational performance indicators (such as database query latency, worker queue backlog, and HTTP 5xx error rates) are monitored server-side through internal audit logs and encrypted metrics pipelines. No tracking cookies are injected into end-user browsers for operational monitoring.

5 Cookie Preferences & Browser Management

You have the ability to control and manage cookies through your web browser preferences:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Enhanced Tracking Protection
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Prevent cross-site tracking
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Important Operational Note: Blocking or disabling strictly necessary session cookies will prevent login to FieldOps Nexus and disrupt all field service and technician workflows.
