FieldOps Nexus Operational Disclaimer

Version 2.0 • Effective: October 2026 • Technical Draft: HPE-PRD-2026-FOPS02

ENG-DISC-2026
This Enterprise System Disclaimer sets forth important technical and operational boundaries regarding FieldOps Nexus software operations, predictive algorithms, sensor integrations, and service availability.

1 General Information & Decision-Support Disclaimer

FieldOps Nexus operates as an Enterprise Asset, Maintenance & Field Service ERP platform. All predictive calculations, Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF), Mean Time to Repair (MTTR), straight-line asset depreciation models, and automated SLA breach risk scores provided by the platform are intended exclusively for operational planning and technical decision support.

The platform does not replace the professional engineering judgment of certified plant engineers, licensed electricians, or certified occupational safety personnel. Equipment operating decisions must be verified on-site in accordance with applicable manufacturer service manuals.

2 Platform Availability & Maintenance Windows

While FieldOps Nexus targets 99.9% operational availability for production tenant workspaces, uninterrupted or error-free platform operation cannot be unconditionally guaranteed:

  • Scheduled Maintenance: Core infrastructure upgrades, database re-indexing, and security patches occur during designated off-peak maintenance windows, announced at least 48 hours in advance via system alerts.
  • Emergency Security Updates: Critical zero-day vulnerability mitigations may require immediate rolling service restarts without advance scheduling.
  • Network & Telecommunications: FieldOps Nexus is not responsible for field connectivity outages, mobile carrier dead-zones, or client local area network failures preventing field tablet synchronization.

3 Accuracy of Information & Field Telemetry

System specifications and operational workflows adhere to verified technical draft standards. However:

  • IoT & Sensor Calibration: Telemetry streams, vibration sensor data, and operating temperatures rely on third-party hardware transducers. FieldOps Nexus does not warrant the physical calibration or hardware accuracy of client-installed IoT devices.
  • Manual Data Entries: Work order notes, technician labor timesheets, and spare parts counts reflect the manual inputs submitted by client personnel. Client organizations remain responsible for verifying data integrity before approving invoice sign-offs.

4 Third-Party Services & Integrations

The platform provides connectors to third-party enterprise services (including SAP, Oracle ERP, geolocation map services, SMS notification gateways, and external object storage). FieldOps Nexus exercises no direct control over the uptime, API deprecation, or data processing policies of these external vendors, and disclaims liability for interruptions attributable to external API downtime.

5 Limitation of Responsibility & Evaluation Gate

Under no circumstances shall FieldOps Nexus, its architects, or engineering personnel be held liable for any direct or indirect loss resulting from industrial machine downtime, production schedule delays, or unexecuted preventive maintenance cycles.

Governance Acceptance Standard:

Final formal acceptance of platform releases and operational calibration is subject to the mandatory 90-calendar-day evaluation gate specified in governance framework HPE-PRD-2026-FOPS02.
